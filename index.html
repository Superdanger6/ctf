About CTF

Our Capture the Flag (CTF) events are designed for cybersecurity enthusiasts to practice and showcase their skills. Solve puzzles, find vulnerabilities, and learn along the way!

Challenges

Explore a variety of challenges:

  • Web Exploitation
  • Cryptography
  • Reverse Engineering
  • Forensics

Rules

Ensure a fair and safe competition:

  • No malicious activities outside the scope of challenges.
  • Respect other participants.
  • Do not share solutions.
  • Have fun and learn!

FAQs

Answers to your most frequently asked questions:

What is a CTF?
A CTF is a cybersecurity competition where participants solve challenges to capture flags.
Who can participate?
Anyone with an interest in cybersecurity, from beginners to experts!
How do I join?
Register on our website and start participating in challenges.
